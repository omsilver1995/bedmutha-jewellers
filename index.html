Our Story

A Nashik Legacy in Gold & Silver

Bedmutha Jewellers has been the family's calling for over two decades — hand-finished gold and silver pieces from a small showroom in Panchavati, Nashik. What began as a quiet family trade has grown into one of the neighbourhood's most-trusted jewellery destinations, guided still by the same idea:

Elegance Is Not Being Noticed, It's About Being Remembered.

Every piece — from a bridal gold set to a delicate silver payal — is chosen for its craft, purity and character. If you can find your way to Adgaon Naka, we would love to show you around the showroom.

— Shital Bedmutha  ·  Serving Nashik since 2004
